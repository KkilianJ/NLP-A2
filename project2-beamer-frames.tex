\begin{frame}[t]{Recurrent Encoders}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\raggedright\footnotesize
\textbf{GloVe}\par\smallskip
\textbf{BiGRU and BiLSTM}\par
One bidirectional layer, 64 units per direction, linear head. Adam 0.001, batch 128, gradient clip 1.0. Max 8 epochs, patience 2. Select by inner-validation macro-F1.

\smallskip
\textbf{Why 256 tokens?}\par
Cap 256 GloVe hits. Inner-validation macro-F1 rose from 84.37\% at 128 to 87.87\% at 256.
